    6. Things to Try

    • Switch to Pooled 1.0 / 1.0 and press Play. The projection follows his hot season much more slowly.
    • Set both weights near 0. The prior almost disappears, and the projection just follows his raw average.
    • Edit games 1–10 to 18 for 28. Watch how little one hot window moves the final estimate.
    • Compare the 95% range at game 10 with the one at game 150. That gap is the cost of having a small sample.

    Input rules

    • Hits and at-bats must be whole numbers, hits ≥ 0, and hits ≤ at-bats.
    • Each prior season needs at least 1 at-bat unless its weight is 0. α₀ and β₀ are floored at 0.5 so the Beta stays valid.
    • Seasons above 700 at-bats get a soft warning. That's more than a realistic full season.
    Model: θ ~ Beta(α₀, β₀); hits | θ ~ Binomial(AB, θ); future hits ~ Beta-Binomial(R, α, β). Remaining at-bats assume the current pace.
